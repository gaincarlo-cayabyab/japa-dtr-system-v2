## How the times were estimated

- Both IN and OUT are rounded **up to the next 10-minute mark**, following the requested example: 8:13 → 8:20. For example, 6:32 PM → 6:40 PM and 10:05 PM → 10:10 PM.
- A time already on a 10-minute mark stays unchanged, such as 8:20 → 8:20.
- Recorded columns retain the clock readings and any visible AM, PM or OT notation. Rounded columns are estimates, not the original punches.
- Where no AM/PM is written, a morning IN is interpreted as AM and an afternoon OUT as PM. The afternoon-only 1:00 IN for Warim is interpreted as PM. These are stated assumptions, not additional information from the cards.
- An unsuffixed 12:00 OUT stays marked **AM/PM unclear**. Explicit 12:00 AM and early-morning time-outs are shown with **(+1 day)**, meaning the following calendar date.
- **?** marks a tentative transcription. **~** before a rounded value means it depends on that tentative reading. **Review** means no reliable rounded value can be assigned. Missing times are **—** and are not invented.
- Printed SUNDAY labels do not prove absence or a rest day. Handwritten Sunday times are included. ABSENT and May sakit are retained only where written.
- The tables show the first recorded IN and final recorded OUT for each attendance date. Additional numeric afternoon IN entries are retained in Notes. Work descriptions in punch columns are not treated as clock times. No lunch break, paid-hours total or overtime duration is assumed.
